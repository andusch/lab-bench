# Export a PNG

## Problem

The student can see the plot, and the lab report has no image of it.

## Outcome

Tauri writes a PNG of the plot from P1-04. The image includes the points, both uncertainties, and the fitted curve.

## Acceptance

- The PNG opens as an image.
- The image shows the points, both uncertainties, and the curve for the current fit.

## Tests

- Export Hooke's law at degree 1 and open the PNG.
- Export the capacitor sample at degree 2 and open the PNG.
- Confirm the file begins with the PNG signature.

- A PDF.
- The Markdown summary.
