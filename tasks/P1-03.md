# Fit weighted least squares

## Problem

The student can see the points, and the desk has no line or polynomial.

## Outcome

Rust fits the series from P1-01. The degree is 1, 2, or 3. The weight of a point is the inverse square of `dy`. The result is the coefficients, a standard error on each coefficient, and R^2. Later tickets use this fit. `dx` stays out of the weight.

## Acceptance

- Degree 1 on `samples/hookes-law.csv` returns a slope between 17.5 and 18.5 N/m. Measured on the current sample, the weighted degree-1 slope is 18.1003 N/m.
- Replacing `dx` and fitting again leaves the slope unchanged.
- Degree 4 is rejected.
- A fit of `samples/capacitor-discharge.csv` has no time constant.

## Tests

- Fit the Hooke's law sample at degree 1 and read the slope.
- Change `dx` on that series, fit again, and compare the slope.
- Request degree 4 and confirm the desk does not replace the current fit.
- Fit the capacitor sample and confirm the result has coefficients, standard errors, and R^2, with no time constant.

## Non-goals

- Drawing the curve.
- Writing a PNG or a Markdown file.
- Levenberg-Marquardt and any named nonlinear curve.
