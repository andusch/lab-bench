# Load a four-column CSV

## Problem

A student has a four-column lab CSV, and the desk has no series to plot.

## Outcome

Tauri opens the file. Rust reads four columns into one series. The series keeps the header names and one point per row, as `(x, dx, y, dy)`. Later tickets use this series. `samples/hookes-law.csv` yields 10 points. `samples/capacitor-discharge.csv` yields 17 points.

## Acceptance

- Opening `samples/hookes-law.csv` yields 10 points, with names from that header.
- Opening `samples/capacitor-discharge.csv` yields 17 points, with names from that header.
- A file with three columns loads nothing.

## Tests

- Load both sample files and count the points.
- Load a three-column CSV and confirm the desk keeps no series from that file.

## Non-goals

- A plot.
- A fit.
- A second header dialect.
- Serial input.
